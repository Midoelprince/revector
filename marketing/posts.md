**Title:**
Show HN: Revector – Free, offline image-to-vector converter (Windows & web, no paywall)

**Text:**
I built Revector because most online image-to-vector converters let you preview a trace and only put up a paywall when you click Download—or require uploading client logos to someone else's server.

Revector is MIT-licensed and runs 100% locally on your machine (Electron Windows app + static site):
- Opens PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, and BMP
- Exports fill-only SVG, EPS, PDF, DXF, and PNG with no watermarks or limits
- Rebuilds small/pixelated images at 4x via signed distance fields, detects geometric primitives (circles, ellipses, rounded rectangles, polygons, stars), and cleans up blurred scans
- Includes a full Arabic (RTL) and English UI

Download (Windows installer & portable): https://github.com/Midoelprince/revector/releases/latest

**Platforms:** Windows (Desktop), Web (Offline Static Site)  
**Alternative to:** Vectorizer.AI, Vector Magic

**Description:**
Revector is a free, open-source, offline image-to-vector converter for Windows. It traces PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, and BMP images into clean, editable SVG, EPS, PDF, and DXF files entirely on your own computer—with no account, no file uploads, no watermarks, and no paywall at download.

Key features:
- Local edge reconstruction for small or pixelated images and blur cleanup for photos/scans
- Exact geometric shape fitting (circles, ellipses, rectangles, rounded rectangles, polygons, stars)
- Shared-border ("cutouts") tracing to prevent hairline gaps between colors, plus stacked layering
- Interactive color palette editor (merge, split, recolor, remove background) and shape removal
- Full English and Arabic (RTL) interface
