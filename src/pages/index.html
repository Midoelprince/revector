What you get back

Revector doesn't just follow the edges of the pixels. It sorts out the colors, finds the corners, and draws each outline with as few points as it can.

True circles and boxes

When an outline is close to a circle, an ellipse, a rectangle or a rounded rectangle, Revector puts in that exact shape. A round dot becomes a real circle you can resize.

Light on anchor points

Straight runs become single lines, curves become smooth Bézier curves, and corners stay where the picture has them. Less to tidy up in Illustrator or Inkscape.

No gaps between colors

Colors that touch are cut along one shared border, so no white hairlines or overlaps show up when you zoom in or print.

How It Works

  1. 01

    Open

    Drop a picture onto the page, paste it, or browse for one. It is read on your computer and never uploaded.

    A PNG or JPEG is a grid of colored squares. Revector reads that grid and works out the shapes behind it.

  2. 02

    Adjust

    A first result appears within seconds, with no settings to choose.

    Compare it with the original, zoom right in, change the number of colors, merge or recolor them, and remove shapes you don't want. Undo is always one click away.

  3. 03

    Save

    Pick a format and save. Every export is free and has no watermark.

    SVG, PDF, EPS and DXF for design, print and cutting machines, or PNG for a cleaned-up picture.

SVG · PNG

Tiny icons and pixelated pictures are smoothed before tracing, so stair-stepped edges come out as clean curves.

Why it runs on your computer

Most converters ask you to upload your picture to their server. Revector does all the work on your own machine.

It works without an internet connection, there is no limit on how many pictures you convert, and client logos or private sketches never pass through anyone else's hands.

Revector is open source under the MIT License, so anyone can read exactly what it does with your files.

FAQ

Is {{BRAND}} really free?

Yes. Converting and saving cost nothing, there is no account to make, and the files have no watermark.

Are my pictures uploaded anywhere?

No. Pictures are read and traced on your computer and are never sent over the internet.

Which files can I open and save?

Open PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP or WebP. Save SVG, PDF, EPS, DXF or PNG.

What kind of pictures work best?

Logos, icons, flat illustrations, cartoons and line drawings with clear colors. Photos can be traced too, but they come out as a poster-style picture with a limited number of colors.

How large can a picture be?

Any size opens. Pictures bigger than 4096 pixels on their longest side are scaled down before tracing, so crop to the part you need to keep the most detail.

Does it keep transparent backgrounds?

Yes. Transparent areas stay empty in the result. You can also make any color transparent, or remove the background with one checkbox.

Can I make files for Cricut, laser cutters or CNC?

Yes. Save SVG for Cricut and vinyl cutters, or DXF for laser and CNC software.

Will the files open in my design software?

They are standard SVG, PDF, EPS and DXF files, which Illustrator, Inkscape, CorelDRAW, Affinity Designer and most cutting programs can open.

Who owns the results?

You do. {{BRAND}} makes no claim on your pictures or the files you save. Just make sure you have the right to use the original picture.

How do I get updates?

The installed app checks for updates when it starts, or click Check for Updates at the top of the window. The portable version doesn't update itself, so download the newest one from the Releases page.
