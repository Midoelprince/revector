FAQ

Is {{BRAND}} free?

Opening, vectorizing, previewing and saving are free. Everything runs on your own computer, with no account and no uploads.

Can I make cut files for laser cutters, Cricut and CNC?

Yes. Download DXF or SVG, and switch on outlines to get thin strokes instead of filled shapes.

How is this different from auto-trace in design software?

Auto-trace tools often follow pixel edges closely and leave lots of points. We group colors first, then smooth and simplify each outline, keeping corners sharp and curves round.

What makes {{BRAND}} stand out?

Shapes are stacked so neighboring colors never leave gaps, you can tune the palette and edit shapes before downloading, and nothing leaves your computer. The algorithm fits circular arcs and other common classes of shapes for clarity at any size.

What file formats can I upload and export?

Upload PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP or WebP. Download SVG, EPS, DXF, PDF or PNG.

What is the maximum image size?

Large pictures are scaled down to 4096 pixels on the longest side before tracing. Since we have a maximum allowable resolution, crop first to keep the most details where you need them.

Does it work with transparent images?

Yes. Transparent areas automatically stay transparent in the vector result, as long as they have no color behind them.

What are vector images useful for?

Vectors scale to any size without blur, so they suit logos, signs, print, cutting machines, embroidery and web graphics.

Will it work on my images?

It works best on logos, flat illustrations and drawings. Photos work too, but come out as posterised artwork.

Does this work on AI-generated images?

Yes. Images from AI-powered generators often have flat, illustrated styles, which give especially clean results.

Will the results work with my software?

The files are standard SVG, PDF, EPS and DXF, which open in common design, office and cutting programs.

Is there a difference between vectorizing and embedding?

Yes. Embedding only wraps the pixels inside a vector file. Vectorizing redraws the picture as real shapes you can edit and scale.

Do you make any claims on the input images or vectorized results?

No. Your pictures and results stay yours.

What are your data retention policies?

Nothing is uploaded. Your picture is processed on your computer and is never sent anywhere.

Can I use your output for machine learning training?

The results are yours to use as you like, as long as you have the rights to the original picture.

How much does it cost?

The desktop converter is free.
